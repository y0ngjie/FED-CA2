How to grow your own Starter Kit

Follow these steps to grow your own mushrooms:

  1. Pour out 4 bags of sawdust (80kg)
  2. Measure & pour Mushroom food and mix together
  3. Mix 1kg lime powder, 0.5kg rice bran together in water and mix together
  4. Pour into sawdust & mix together with shovel
  5. Add 500g gypsum & 500g calcium rock powder together in water and mix them
  6. Pour the mixture into the pile and mix
  7. Add enough water to form a clump of mixture
  8. Use a scoop to put mixture into plastic bag
  9. Cover the plastic with a cap to ensure bag is air-tight
  10. Steam the mushroom spores for approx. 10 to prevent contamination from other fungi
  11. Add starter culture into the mushroom spore after creating the sterile environment
  12. The mushrooms will grow after 10-15 days. Enjoy your growing process!

Your Starter Kit Instructions

Follow these steps to use your mushroom starter kit:

  1. Open your kit and check all components are present: growing substrate, mushroom spores, spray bottle.
  2. Carefully mix the mushroom spores into the substrate according to the package instructions.
  3. Place the mixture in the provided container and mist lightly with water.
  4. Keep in a dark, warm place (around 70°F or 21°C) for about 2 weeks until you see white mycelium growth.
  5. Once growth is visible, move to a cooler area with indirect light and continue to mist daily.
  6. Harvest your mushrooms when the caps are fully opened, typically 1-2 weeks after first appearance.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Common Issues:

  • No growth after 3 weeks: Check temperature and moisture levels. Ensure the environment isn't too dry.
  • Mold growth: If you see green or black mold, unfortunately, you'll need to dispose of the kit and start over.
  • Small or stunted mushrooms: This usually indicates insufficient moisture. Increase misting frequency.

FAQ:

Q: How long until I see mushrooms?
A: Typically, you'll see the first signs of growth in 2-3 weeks, with harvestable mushrooms in 4-6 weeks.

Q: Can I reuse my kit?
A: Yes! After your first harvest, you can often get a second or even third flush of mushrooms.

Mushroom Care Tips

Optimal Growing Conditions:

  • Temperature: 55-75°F (13-24°C), depending on mushroom variety
  • Humidity: 80-90%
  • Light: Indirect or low light is best

Harvesting Tips:

  • Harvest when the mushroom caps are fully opened but before they start to curl upwards
  • Use a sharp knife to cut the stem close to the base
  • Store fresh mushrooms in a paper bag in the refrigerator for up to a week

Safety First

While mushroom growing is generally safe, it's important to keep these precautions in mind:

  • Always wash your hands before and after handling your mushroom kit.
  • Keep your growing area clean and free from contaminants.
  • Never consume mushrooms that you cannot positively identify.
  • If you notice any unusual colors or smells, dispose of the kit safely.
  • Keep your kit out of reach of children and pets.

Mushroom Growing 101

The Science Behind Mushroom Growing

Mushrooms are the fruiting bodies of certain fungi. They grow from spores, not seeds, and feed on organic matter rather than using photosynthesis like plants.

A Brief History of Mushroom Cultivation

Humans have been cultivating mushrooms for thousands of years, with the first recorded cultivation in China around 600 AD. Today, mushroom cultivation is a multi-billion dollar global industry!
